# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be considerate, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, make uncertainty explicit, respect privacy, and welcome correction. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, sustained disruption, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact in `SECURITY.md`, clearly marking the report as a conduct matter. Maintainers may warn, moderate, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove participants based on impact and pattern. Good-faith reports and appeals will be handled confidentially and without retaliation.
