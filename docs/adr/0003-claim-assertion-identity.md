# ADR 0003: Claims and assertions have separate identities

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-01

## Decision

A claim identifies a scope-qualified proposition. An assertion identifies an actor's stance toward it at a time and may cite a source fragment. Sources asserting a claim never become Noemosyne assessments implicitly.

## Consequences

Multiple actors may assert or deny one claim without duplicating proposition identity, and attribution survives changes in Noemosyne's own assessment.
